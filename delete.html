萬能記帳本 刪除資料說明

應用程式：萬能記帳本（Google Play）／開發者：CHENG HUNG KAI

萬能記帳本不需要註冊帳號，你的帳本資料只存在你自己的手機裡，開發者看不到也沒有保存。所以大部分資料你可以直接自己刪除，不用等我們處理。

一、刪除手機裡的資料（自己就能完成）

  1. 刪除部分資料：在 APP 裡直接刪除單筆紀錄、帳本、照片、班表或記事。
  2. 刪除全部資料：到手機「設定 › 應用程式 › 萬能記帳本 › 儲存空間 › 清除資料」，或直接解除安裝 APP。帳本、照片、設定、密碼鎖設定都會一起刪除，無法復原。
  3. 備份與匯出檔：你自己存到其他地方的備份檔、Excel、CSV、PDF，請到存放的位置自行刪除。

二、請開發者刪除的資料

開發者唯一可能持有的，是你主動用「問題回報／建議」寄來的電子郵件（內容、附件照片或影片、你同意附上的診斷資訊與錯誤紀錄）。

  1. 用寄出回報的同一個信箱，寫信到 kaisever7916@gmail.com。
  2. 主旨寫「萬能記帳本 刪除資料」。
  3. 我們會在 30 天內刪除你寄來的所有回報郵件與附件，並回信確認。

三、刪除與保留的資料類型

  • 會刪除：手機裡的所有帳本與設定（依第一節）、你寄來的回報郵件與附件（依第二節）。
  • 不會保留：開發者沒有伺服器，不保存你的帳本、位置或任何其他資料。
  • 由 Google 保存：Google Play 的購買紀錄由 Google 依其政策保存，開發者無法刪除。廣告識別碼可在手機「設定 › Google › 廣告」重設或刪除。
  • 保留期限：沒有提出刪除要求的回報郵件，處理完畢後 1 年內刪除。

Wanneng Ledger: Deleting Your Data

App: Wanneng Ledger (萬能記帳本) on Google Play / Developer: CHENG HUNG KAI

Wanneng Ledger has no accounts. Your books are stored only on your own phone; the developer cannot see them and keeps no copy. You can delete most data yourself right away.

1. Delete data on your phone (do it yourself)

  1. Delete some data: Delete individual records, books, photos, schedules or notes in the app.
  2. Delete everything: Go to your phone's Settings › Apps › Wanneng Ledger › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. All books, photos, settings and passcode lock settings are deleted and cannot be recovered.
  3. Backups and exports: Delete any backup, Excel, CSV or PDF files you saved elsewhere yourself.

2. Ask the developer to delete data

The only data the developer may hold is report emails you chose to send with "Report a problem / Suggestion" (your text, attached photos or videos, and the diagnostics and error log you agreed to include).

  1. From the email address you used to send the report, write to kaisever7916@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Wanneng Ledger data deletion".
  3. We delete all your report emails and attachments within 30 days and reply to confirm.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: All books and settings on your phone (section 1), and your report emails and attachments (section 2).
  • Not kept: The developer has no server and keeps none of your books, location or other data.
  • Kept by Google: Google Play purchase records are kept by Google under its own policy and cannot be deleted by the developer. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Retention: Report emails without a deletion request are deleted within 1 year after handling.
