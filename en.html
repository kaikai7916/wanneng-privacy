Wanneng Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 (version 1.0)

1. Overview

Your books stay on your phone. We cannot see or store what you record. This policy explains how Wanneng Ledger ("the app") handles your data. The app is provided by an individual developer, CHENG HUNG KAI.

The app does not require an account. It does not collect your name, phone number, email address or ID number, and it never sells any data.

2. Data that stays on your phone

The following data is stored only on your phone. The developer cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to any server:

  • Entries, categories, budgets, accounts, trip and baby books, currency exchanges, price comparison lists
  • Accounting books (company details, tax ID, vouchers, accounts) and stock books (trades, holdings, regular investment plans)
  • Receipt data, shift schedules, clinic rosters (doctor names and session times), calendar notes, alarms
  • Your baby's doctor visit records (date, doctor, symptoms, advice)
  • Photos you take or choose (receipts, products, documents, medicine bags)
  • App settings and passcode lock settings

Your passcode or pattern is stored only as an irreversible hash in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore). When you unlock with your fingerprint or face, recognition is done by the phone's system; the app only receives "success" or "failure" and never gets your fingerprint or face data.

To keep your books from being uploaded unencrypted, the app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup. To move to a new phone, use the encrypted backup in the app.

6. Your rights, retention and deletion

All your data is on your own phone, so you can exercise your rights directly in the app without contacting us.

  • Access and copy: In More › Backup & export, export Excel, CSV or PDF, or create an encrypted backup with all your data. Backups are encrypted with your password, which we cannot recover for you.
  • Correct: Edit any record directly in the app.
  • Delete: Delete individual records, books or photos in the app. To delete everything, go to your phone's Settings › Apps › Wanneng Ledger › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. Delete any backup or export files you saved elsewhere yourself.
  • Stop ad tracking: See section 5, or buy Remove Ads.
  • Report emails: Email kaisever7916@gmail.com to ask about or delete report emails you sent.

Retention: Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Report emails are deleted within 1 year after handling.

7. Other

  • Children: The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data. Baby books and visit records are kept by parents and stay on the parent's phone.
  • Health information: Clinic rosters and visit records are only your own notes. They are not uploaded and are not medical advice; always follow your doctor's diagnosis.
  • Disclaimer: Exchange rates, stock prices, lottery numbers and sales tax estimates are for reference only; rely on official announcements. The app does not give investment advice or file taxes for you.
  • Future features: Shared books, automatic Google Drive backup and recording from card notifications are not available yet. Before adding them, we will update this policy and notify you in the app.
  • Changes: If this policy changes significantly, we will notify you in the app and update the effective date above.
